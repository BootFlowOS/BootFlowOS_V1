# BootFlow OS Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming, and technically constructive community for people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate respectfully and assume good faith.
- Focus technical disagreements on evidence, implementation, safety, and project goals.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Respect privacy, licensing, and responsible-security boundaries.
- Help newer contributors learn without ridicule.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, stalking, doxxing, deliberate disruption, sexualized conduct, publishing another person's private information, and encouraging unauthorized system access are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [BootFlowOS@Proton.me](mailto:BootFlowOS@Proton.me). Include links or screenshots when possible, but do not collect or publish unnecessary personal information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock conversations, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Scope

This policy applies to BootFlow OS repositories, Issues, Discussions, pull requests, project-managed communication spaces, and public representation of the project.
